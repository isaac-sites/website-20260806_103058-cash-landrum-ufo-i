## Introduction

The [administrative claims]({{ 'claims-process/' | relative_url }}) filed by Betty Cash, [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}) and Colby Landrum did not establish that the United States Air Force was responsible for the alleged Cash–Landrum incident. Their legal importance lay elsewhere. By presenting formal monetary claims to the Air Force, receiving written denials and pursuing the available administrative review, the witnesses satisfied the procedural requirements that generally had to be completed before bringing a damages action against the United States under the Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA). Once that administrative process ended unsuccessfully, the courthouse door became available to them—not because the government had accepted their account, but because the law required the agency to be given the first opportunity to consider and resolve the claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/document/908709170/126065-r" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[scribd.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Federal Tort Claims Act Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Federal Tort Claims Act Case Summary &#124; PDF &#124; Lawsuit &#124; Diseases And Disorders...</span></span></span>

## Why a Monetary Demand Had to Come First

The witnesses sought compensation from the federal government for alleged personal injuries, medical expenses and related losses. Claims of this type against the United States fall under the FTCA, which waives sovereign immunity only on specified conditions. One of the most important conditions is administrative exhaustion.

Under 28 U.S.C. § 2675(a), a claimant cannot begin an FTCA lawsuit until the appropriate federal agency has first received the claim and either:

* issued a written final denial; or
* failed to make a final decision within six months, allowing the claimant to treat the inaction as a constructive denial.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/508/106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: law.cornell.edu">[cornell.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">law.cornell.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cornell.edu.</span></span></span>

The Bergstrom Air Force Base legal office therefore directed the witnesses towards the formal claims procedure rather than immediately towards litigation. The recorded interview, supporting documents and claim forms became part of an administrative process designed to determine whether the government would voluntarily pay compensation before judicial proceedings became necessary. The Air Force's willingness to process the paperwork did not imply agreement that military aircraft or personnel had caused the alleged injuries. Instead, it reflected the statutory procedure governing tort claims against the federal government.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Transcript, Cash-Landrum InterviewJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

## How Denial and Appeal Completed the Agency Process

The administrative stage unfolded over many months. After assembling [medical records]({{ 'medical-record/' | relative_url }}), witness statements and monetary demands, the three claimants submitted formal compensation claims to the Department of the Air Force.

The agency ultimately rejected those claims. Rather than ending the matter immediately, the claimants pursued the available administrative appeal. That appeal was also unsuccessful. Contemporary investigators involved with the case later noted that military legal personnel advised the witnesses that, once the administrative remedies had been exhausted, the appropriate next step would be a civil action in federal court.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Magazine/M/MUFON%20UFO%20Journal/MUFON%20UFO%20Journal%20-%20October%201986.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: electronicsandbooks.com">[Electronics and Books]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">electronicsandbooks.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MUFON UFO Journal October 1986</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Electronics and BooksMUFON UFO JOURNALFebruary 5, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 1986</span></span></span>

The significance of these denials is sometimes misunderstood. They did not represent a judicial determination that the reported encounter never occurred, nor did they establish that the witnesses were mistaken. Instead, they marked the completion of the executive branch's handling of the monetary claims. Once the agency's decision became final, the procedural barrier preventing an FTCA lawsuit was removed. That distinction is fundamental to understanding the later litigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/508/106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: law.cornell.edu">[Legal Information Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">law.cornell.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cornell.edu.</span></span></span>

## How the Administrative Record Became the Bridge to Federal Court

The federal complaint filed on behalf of Cash, Landrum and Colby explicitly relied upon completion of the administrative process. The pleading stated that the claims had first been presented to the Department of the Air Force, that the department issued a final denial on 2 September 1983, and that the lawsuit was filed within the statutory period following that denial. Those allegations were not incidental—they established the court's ability to consider the FTCA action at all.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/document/908709170/126065-r" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Federal Tort Claims Act Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Federal Tort Claims Act Case Summary &#124; PDF &#124; Lawsuit &#124; Diseases And Disorders...</span></span></span>

The administrative record also became the evidential foundation for the lawsuit. Materials developed before litigation included:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* recorded witness interviews;
* correspondence with military claims officials;
* medical documentation supporting the alleged injuries;
* the written monetary demands;
* the Air Force's formal denial letters.

</div>

These documents did not prove government liability, but they defined the factual allegations and legal theories that were later examined in court. They also demonstrated that the plaintiffs had complied with the statutory sequence required for suits against the United States.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSM...</span></span></span>

## Administrative Exhaustion Did Not Resolve the Central Legal Problem

Completing the administrative process solved one procedural obstacle but left the principal evidential obstacle untouched.

The FTCA permits recovery only where the alleged injury was caused by a federal employee acting within the scope of official duties. Throughout both the administrative proceedings and the later litigation, the government consistently denied possessing or operating the alleged craft and denied involvement by military helicopters matching the witnesses' descriptions. Because the plaintiffs ultimately could not establish that [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }}) or accompanying helicopters belonged to or were controlled by the United States, the courts concluded that the essential element of government responsibility had not been proven.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.upi.com/Archives/1985/09/03/Three-suing-government-over-UFO-radiation/1920494568000/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upi.com">[upi.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Three suing government over UFO radiation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Three suing government over UFO radiation - UPI ArchivesSeptember 3, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 3, 1985</span></span></span>

In other words, administrative exhaustion answered only the question, "May the plaintiffs sue?" It did not answer the much harder question, "Can they prove the United States is legally responsible?"

## The Lasting Procedural Significance

Viewed narrowly, the administrative claims failed: no compensation was awarded, and the Air Force denied liability.

Viewed procedurally, however, they accomplished exactly what federal law required. They transformed informal complaints into formal legal claims, generated an administrative record, produced final agency decisions and satisfied the exhaustion requirement that allowed the witnesses to invoke federal jurisdiction under the FTCA. Without those completed administrative steps, the Cash–Landrum plaintiffs would likely have faced dismissal before any court considered the substance of their allegations. The exhaustion process therefore served as the essential bridge between their unsuccessful claims before the Air Force and the federal lawsuit that followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/508/106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: law.cornell.edu">[cornell.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">law.cornell.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cornell.edu.</span></span></span>

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
