## Introduction

Before the Cash–Landrum witnesses filed a federal lawsuit, they first tried to obtain help through their elected representatives. [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}) wrote to Texas Senators Lloyd Bentsen and John Tower seeking assistance after the reported December 1980 encounter and the illnesses she believed followed it. The senators did not investigate the UFO claim, endorse the witnesses' account or accept that the United States military was involved. Instead, they performed a more limited but important function: they identified the appropriate government office for a damages claim and directed Cash into the Air Force's established [administrative claims]({{ 'claims-process/' | relative_url }}) system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSMJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

That distinction is central to understanding the administrative phase of the case. Congressional offices acted as intermediaries, helping a constituent reach the correct federal agency. Their referrals opened an official route for compensation claims, but they did not constitute evidence that Congress or the Department of Defense believed the reported object belonged to the military.

## What Betty Cash Asked Senators Bentsen and Tower

By mid-1981, Cash had contacted several public figures in search of official assistance. During her later recorded interview at Bergstrom Air Force Base, she explained that investigator Allan Hendry had encouraged her to write to members of Congress. She sent letters to Senator Lloyd Bentsen and Senator John Tower describing the incident and asking for help.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

The surviving correspondence shows different but complementary responses:

* **Lloyd Bentsen** consulted Department of Defense representatives and advised Cash to contact the Judge Advocate Claims Officer at Bergstrom Air Force Base to file an official report and submit a claim for damages. His office also informed her that Bergstrom personnel had been alerted to her situation before her arrival.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSMJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>
* **John Tower** likewise directed her to the Base Staff Judge Advocate and Claims Officer at Bergstrom, providing the appropriate contact information for pursuing an administrative claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSMJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

Neither letter purported to determine what the witnesses had seen. Instead, both addressed the procedural question of where a citizen alleging injury by federal activity should present a claim.

## Why Congressional Help Meant Access, Not Endorsement

A common misunderstanding is that the senators' involvement represented political support for the UFO allegations. The documentary record indicates something much narrower.

Members of Congress routinely help constituents navigate federal agencies by requesting information, identifying the office with jurisdiction or encouraging an agency to respond. They do not determine liability, investigate technical evidence or order compensation simply because a constituent has contacted them.

In the Cash–Landrum case, the senators' actions reflected that normal constituent-service role. By directing Cash to the Air Force claims office, they recognised that if the alleged injuries had been caused by military activity, the Air Force was the appropriate agency to receive a claim. That procedural referral should not be interpreted as congressional validation of the witnesses' account or as acknowledgement that military aircraft were involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSMJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

This distinction later became significant in court. Although the witnesses had been guided into the official claims process, they still bore the burden of proving that the United States government was responsible for the incident.

## How the Referrals Led to Bergstrom Air Force Base

The senators' correspondence directly resulted in the witnesses travelling to Bergstrom Air Force Base near Austin.

On 17 August 1981, Betty Cash, [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}) and [Colby Landrum]({{ 'colby-landrum/' | relative_url }}) met Air Force legal personnel in the base law library. Present were Captain John Camp, Acting Staff Judge Advocate; Captain Terry Davis, the Claims Officer; and Pat Wolf, Assistant Claims Officer. The meeting was recorded, creating one of the most important primary documents in the administrative history of the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

The transcript itself confirms the chain of events. Early in the interview, Camp asked why Cash had come to Bergstrom, and she explained that Senator Bentsen had advised her to contact the Judge Advocate at the Claims Office. Camp requested a copy of the senator's letter for the Air Force files before proceeding with questions about the reported encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

The meeting therefore existed because the congressional referrals had successfully connected the witnesses with the Air Force's administrative claims process.

The Bergstrom officials treated the meeting as a legal claims matter rather than a UFO investigation.

## Why the Senator Referrals Matter in the Administrative Timeline

The senators' involvement occupies a specific place in the chronology between informal complaints and formal litigation.

Without their guidance, Cash may not have identified the Air Force claims office responsible for receiving allegations of government-caused injury. Their intervention transformed private correspondence into an official administrative proceeding, creating:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a documented referral from elected representatives;
* a recorded Air Force interview with the three witnesses;
* the filing of formal administrative claims; and
* the procedural foundation required before later litigation against the United States could proceed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlanC.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cashlan C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">C U F O NSMJune 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
