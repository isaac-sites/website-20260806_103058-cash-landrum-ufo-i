## Introduction

One of the most memorable details in [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }})'s account of the Cash–Landrum incident is her claim that the unidentified object heated her car so intensely that she could not grasp the driver's door handle with her bare hand. She said she wrapped the bottom of her leather jacket around her hand to open the door after standing outside near [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }}). The story has become one of the case's strongest physical claims because it suggests that the reported heat affected not only the witnesses but also their vehicle. At the same time, it illustrates one of the central evidential problems in the case: the reported overheating was never documented through controlled forensic examination. As a result, the claim remains an important part of the witness testimony but not independently verified physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

## Did the Object Really Overheat Betty Cash's Car?

Cash first described the overheated door handle during her recorded interview with U.S. Air Force personnel in August 1981. According to the transcript, she left the vehicle to observe the brilliant object and soon found the heat unbearable. When she attempted to return to [the car]({{ 'the-car/' | relative_url }}), she stated that the metal handle was so hot that she had to use the pocket or lower edge of her leather jacket to pull the door open. She also reported that the car's engine temporarily stopped functioning while the object remained nearby.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

No independent witness measured the temperature of the vehicle, photographed the handle immediately afterwards or preserved it for later examination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

## Cash's Door Handle Account

The door-handle claim serves an important role within the overall narrative because it bridges subjective experience and alleged physical effects.

According to Cash's testimony:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* She stood outside the vehicle for several minutes while observing the object.
* The heat became so intense that she decided to retreat.
* She could not grip the exterior door handle directly.
* She used her leather jacket as insulation before opening the door.
* Once inside, she said the car itself felt extremely hot.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

</div>

This sequence is internally consistent with her broader description of intense thermal exposure. It also explains why later investigators treated the vehicle as potentially important evidence rather than simply a means of transport.

Some later retellings expand the story by describing severe burns from the handle itself. The earliest interview transcript is more restrained. Cash described the handle as painfully hot to touch, requiring protection from her jacket, rather than explicitly claiming that the handle burned her hand.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

## What Physical Testing Was Missing?

The principal weakness of the overheated-car claim is not necessarily that it is impossible, but that the evidence was never collected in a way that allows independent evaluation.

Investigators did not have access to:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* documented temperature measurements taken shortly after the encounter;
* laboratory analysis of the metal door handle;
* metallurgical examination for abnormal heat effects;
* controlled inspection of paint, weather seals, glass or trim for unusual thermal damage;
* a documented chain of custody preserving the vehicle as evidence.

</div>

Without these forms of examination, it is impossible to determine whether the car experienced temperatures outside those encountered in more ordinary circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

Some reports later referred to softened dashboard vinyl or an apparent handprint left by [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}). Although these claims became widely repeated in UFO literature, comprehensive forensic documentation of the vehicle has never entered the public record. ## Heat Testimony Versus Verified Damage

The distinction between testimony and verified physical evidence is especially important in this aspect of the Cash–Landrum case.

Cash consistently maintained that the vehicle became dangerously hot, and her account remained broadly similar across interviews. That consistency gives historians and investigators a stable description of what she believed happened. Consistency, however, is not the same as independent verification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

From an evidential standpoint, investigators would ideally expect several observable consequences if a vehicle had been exposed to heat intense enough to make exterior metal painful to touch:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* measurable alteration of paint or protective coatings;
* deformation or discolouration of plastic trim;
* documented thermal effects on rubber components;
* evidence of uneven heating corresponding to the object's reported position.

</div>

The public record does not contain laboratory findings demonstrating such effects. Nor has any published forensic report concluded that the vehicle exhibited damage requiring an extraordinary heat source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

This does not prove that Cash's experience was fabricated or mistaken. Instead, it means that one of the incident's strongest apparent physical claims cannot be assessed using the kind of objective testing normally expected in investigations involving alleged thermal exposure.

## Why the Claim Remains Significant

The overheated-car account remains one of the defining features of the Cash–Landrum incident because it moves beyond a simple visual sighting. If accurate, it would indicate that the reported object interacted physically with its surroundings rather than merely appearing in the sky.

At the same time, it illustrates the broader evidential challenge that runs through the entire case. The witnesses described dramatic physical effects, yet the available documentation consists primarily of testimony, later photographs and recollections rather than contemporaneous forensic examination. Consequently, the overheated door handle remains an important witness statement that supports Cash's description of intense heat, but it does not constitute independently verified proof that the vehicle was exposed to an anomalous thermal source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noufors.com/Documents/Transcript%20of%20taped%20interview%20held%20at%20Bergstom%20Air%20Force%20Base%20Law%20Library%2C%2017%20August%201981.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noufors.com">[noufors.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noufors.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 10, 2002...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2002</span></span></span>

    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
