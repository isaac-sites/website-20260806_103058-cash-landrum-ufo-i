## Introduction

The [medical records]({{ 'medical-record/' | relative_url }}) associated with [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}) after the Cash–Landrum incident document a genuine and significant illness. They record facial and scalp swelling, crusting skin changes, eyelid oedema, pain, prolonged hospitalisation and, later, substantial hair loss. What they do **not** document is a contemporaneous medical diagnosis of acute radiation syndrome or verified radiation exposure. That distinction became increasingly blurred in later retellings, where documented clinical findings were often presented as proof of radiation injury rather than symptoms requiring further explanation. Contemporary records and later medical commentary therefore need to be separated carefully: one establishes that Cash was ill, while the other advances competing interpretations of why she became ill.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I. ## What clinicians directly observed

The strongest evidence in this part of the case comes from the hospital records themselves rather than later summaries.

On admission, physicians documented:

* Marked swelling of the scalp, forehead and face.
* Crusting and inflammation affecting the scalp and eyelids.
* Tenderness of the scalp and surrounding tissues.
* Severe headache and eye complaints.
* Subsequent development of extensive [alopecia]({{ 'alopecia/' | relative_url }}) during later treatment rather than at initial admission.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I. These are objective clinical observations. They do not depend on belief in the reported UFO encounter and demonstrate that Cash required repeated hospital care. Their existence is generally accepted by both supporters and sceptics of the case.

Equally important is what the records did **not** contain. They did not identify a measured radiation dose, document radioactive contamination, or record the characteristic laboratory findings that physicians normally seek when evaluating suspected acute radiation syndrome. Modern clinical guidance emphasises [serial blood counts]({{ 'blood-counts-a88e65/' | relative_url }}), particularly lymphocyte depletion, dose estimation and exposure history when diagnosing acute radiation syndrome.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/ars.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdc.gov">[CDC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acute Radiation Syndrome: Information for Clinicians &#124; Radiation Emergencies &#124; CDC...</span></span></span>

## What diagnoses were actually recorded?

The distinction between recorded diagnosis and later interpretation is central to understanding the medical evidence.

According to the surviving hospital material reproduced by later researchers, consulting dermatologist Dr Solomon Brickman diagnosed **[cellulitis]({{ 'cellulitis/' | relative_url }}) with secondary oedema of the scalp and face**. Treatment included antibiotics and steroids, consistent with managing inflammatory skin disease rather than a confirmed radiation injury.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I. Case Studies</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">I. Case StudiesJune 16, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 16, 2025</span></span></span>

Likewise, admitting physician Dr V. B. Shenoy documented severe swelling and discomfort but reportedly noted little, if any, hair loss at the initial admission. Pronounced alopecia developed later during the course of treatment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I. None of these diagnoses excluded radiation as a theoretical possibility. However, they also did not identify radiation as the established cause.

## Why later writers connected the illness with radiation

The association with radiation developed because several of Cash's symptoms resemble features that can occur after sufficiently intense exposure.

Modern radiation medicine recognises that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Swelling and skin injury can appear after high-dose exposure.
* Hair loss may develop days or weeks after irradiation.
* Skin injury and epilation may evolve gradually rather than immediately.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/cri.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdc.gov">[CDC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cutaneous Radiation Injury (CRI): Information for Clinicians &#124; Radiation Emergencies &#124; CDCApril 17, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2024</span></span></span>

</div>

This overlap encouraged later authors to describe the illness as "[radiation sickness]({{ 'radiation-claim/' | relative_url }})". However, similarity of symptoms is not the same as diagnostic confirmation.

The CDC notes that acute radiation syndrome has no single unique symptom and must be diagnosed by combining clinical history with laboratory evidence, estimated dose, serial blood counts and specialist assessment. Many illnesses can imitate portions of the syndrome without being caused by ionising radiation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/ars.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdc.gov">[CDC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acute Radiation Syndrome: Information for Clinicians &#124; Radiation Emergencies &#124; CDC...</span></span></span>

## Where the wording became stronger than the records

As the Cash–Landrum story spread through books, documentaries and UFO literature, descriptions often shifted from cautious medical language to more definitive phrasing.

A typical progression was:

Contemporary recordLater characterisationSwelling, crusting and cellulitis were documented."Radiation burns" became common terminology.Hair loss developed during later treatment.Hair loss was presented as classic proof of radiation exposure.Radiation remained one possible explanation.Radiation was increasingly described as the established diagnosis.

This evolution illustrates how narratives can harden over time. The underlying medical observations remained largely unchanged, while the explanatory language became more confident than the original clinical documentation justified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I. ## Why verification remained elusive

A confirmed diagnosis of acute radiation syndrome normally rests on more than symptoms alone.

Current clinical guidance expects evidence such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* documented exposure circumstances compatible with a high radiation dose;
* characteristic changes in blood counts, particularly lymphocyte depletion;
* dose reconstruction or dosimetry where possible;
* specialist evaluation of skin injury and systemic effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/ars.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdc.gov">[CDC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acute Radiation Syndrome: Information for Clinicians &#124; Radiation Emergencies &#124; CDC...</span></span></span>

</div>

The published material from Betty Cash's medical care does not demonstrate this chain of confirmation.

Later reviews have also pointed to reports that subsequent investigations, including bone marrow studies, did not reveal findings expected after severe whole-body ionising radiation injury. Researchers have additionally noted references to biopsy findings interpreted as compatible with [alopecia areata]({{ 'alopecia-areata/' | relative_url }}), an autoimmune cause of hair loss, illustrating that clinicians continued to consider non-radiation explanations during her medical course.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gpposner.com/Cash-Landrum-chapter.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gpposner.com">[Gary P. Posner]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gpposner.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gpposner.com.</span></span></span>

## What this distinction means for the Cash–Landrum case

The medical evidence is strongest when describing **what happened to Betty Cash**, not **why it happened**.

The documented findings establish that she experienced a serious illness involving inflammatory skin changes, swelling and later alopecia requiring repeated hospitalisation. Those observations are supported by surviving medical records and are not in serious dispute.

The claim that these findings constitute verified radiation sickness is a separate proposition requiring evidence beyond the symptoms themselves. Contemporary diagnoses did not make that conclusion, and the published medical record lacks the laboratory confirmation and exposure measurements normally used to establish acute radiation syndrome. As a result, the illness itself is well documented, whereas its attribution to ionising radiation remains an interpretation rather than a medically verified diagnosis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10581488/files/Reliability_I-12_Posner.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[zenodo.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">I.     if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
