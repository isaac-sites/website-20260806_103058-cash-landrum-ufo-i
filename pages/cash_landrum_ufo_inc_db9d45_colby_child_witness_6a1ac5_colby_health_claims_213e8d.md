## Introduction

[Colby Landrum]({{ 'colby-landrum/' | relative_url }})'s reported illness is one of the most discussed aspects of the Cash–Landrum incident because it appears to extend the claimed health effects beyond the two adult witnesses to a seven-year-old child. According to Colby and his grandmother, he developed stomach pain, diarrhoea, a small patch of [hair loss]({{ 'hair-loss/' | relative_url }}), blisters and persistent eye irritation after the roadside encounter. Those accounts have remained broadly consistent across later interviews and are part of the historical record of the case. What they do **not** establish, however, is a medically verified cause. The surviving evidence documents symptoms that were reported by the family, but it does not demonstrate that doctors independently concluded they resulted from the alleged encounter, radiation exposure or any other specific mechanism.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2013/07/from-their-own-lips-betty-cash-colby.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: From their own lips: Betty, Colby &amp; Vickie tell their storyJuly 23, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 23, 2013</span></span></span>

## What symptoms did Colby actually report?

The most important primary source for Colby's own account is the recorded interview conducted at Bergstrom Air Force Base in August 1981. Unlike many later retellings, the interview allows readers to separate the child's own statements from later interpretations.

Colby stated that he did not become ill immediately. Instead, he said that the following morning he experienced stomach pain and diarrhoea. He also described developing a small bald patch, blisters and discomfort in his eyes, particularly when exposed to bright sunlight or chlorinated swimming pools. These descriptions are significant because they come directly from Colby rather than being second-hand summaries by investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2013/07/from-their-own-lips-betty-cash-colby.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: From their own lips: Betty, Colby &amp; Vickie tell their storyJuly 23, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 23, 2013</span></span></span>

Later interviews, recorded several years after the incident, repeated a similar pattern. Colby recalled vomiting, blistering and being treated by his grandmother after returning home. Although the wording varied over time, the general sequence of gastrointestinal illness followed by skin and eye complaints remained largely consistent in his recollections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

Consistency, however, is not the same as medical confirmation. Witness testimony establishes what Colby and his guardian believed happened, not why those symptoms occurred.

## Guardian reports versus clinical findings

Much of the stronger language surrounding Colby's condition comes not from Colby himself but from his grandmother, [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}). During media interviews she described bowel problems, blistering, hair loss and concerns that doctors had discussed the possibility of radiation exposure. In some interviews she also repeated warnings that Colby might later develop a serious illness if radiation had been involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

Those claims require careful separation from the available [medical record]({{ 'medical-record/' | relative_url }}).

One important complication is that [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}) received extensive hospital treatment, whereas documentation for comparable medical examination of Colby is far more limited. Researchers reviewing the surviving records note that later interviews often blurred distinctions between Betty's documented treatment and the symptoms reported for Vickie and Colby. In several media appearances, interviewers assumed all three had undergone similar examinations when that was not the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

The frequently repeated statement that doctors diagnosed Colby with radiation poisoning is also difficult to support from contemporary evidence. The radiologist most often cited in later UFO literature, Dr Peter Rank, did not personally examine Colby. Instead, he reviewed available material relating primarily to Betty Cash and offered opinions by correspondence. Even in those communications, he expressed caution and did not present a definitive diagnosis of radiation injury affecting Colby.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2018/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: 2018...</span></span></span>

Similarly, photographs said to document Colby's hair loss show only a small isolated bald patch rather than widespread hair loss. Critics have argued that such photographs do not by themselves establish radiation injury, while supporters regard them as consistent with the family's account. The images cannot resolve the underlying medical question.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

## Why causation remains unresolved

The central uncertainty is not whether Colby [reported symptoms]({{ 'reported-symptoms/' | relative_url }})—he clearly did—but whether those symptoms can be linked to the roadside encounter.

Several factors prevent a firm conclusion:

## How historians and investigators assess the health claims

Modern discussions of the Cash–Landrum incident increasingly distinguish between **reported symptoms** and **verified diagnosis**. This distinction is especially important for Colby because he was a young child whose medical history is known largely through family testimony rather than comprehensive clinical records.

From a historical perspective, Colby's illness remains relevant because it formed part of the family's consistent narrative from an early stage and influenced subsequent legal and investigative efforts. From a medical perspective, however, the available evidence is insufficient to determine whether his stomach pain, hair loss, blisters and eye irritation shared a single cause, were related to the reported encounter, or arose from unrelated conditions.

The result is an enduring uncertainty: the record supports the claim that Colby and his grandmother believed he became ill after the event, but it does not provide independent medical evidence capable of establishing what caused those symptoms.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2013/07/from-their-own-lips-betty-cash-colby.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: From their own lips: Betty, Colby &amp; Vickie tell their storyJuly 23, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 23, 2013</span></span></span>
