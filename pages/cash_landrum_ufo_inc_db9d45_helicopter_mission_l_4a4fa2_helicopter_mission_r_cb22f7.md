## Introduction

One of the central logistical questions raised by the reported helicopter activity in the Cash–Landrum incident is not simply whether approximately 23 military helicopters could have flown together, but what documentary evidence such an operation would normally leave behind. In organised military aviation, aircraft movements are recorded through multiple overlapping administrative, maintenance and operational systems. These systems exist for safety, accountability, maintenance scheduling, crew qualification, fuel management and readiness reporting. As a result, the disappearance or absence of a single record would not normally erase all evidence of a large helicopter mission.

This does not mean every flight leaves a complete public record, nor that all military records remain permanently available. However, a coordinated mission involving dozens of aircraft would ordinarily generate documentation across several independent offices and functions. That expectation helps explain why investigators examining the Cash–Landrum case considered the documentary trail to be almost as important as eyewitness testimony.

## Aircraft and Maintenance Documentation

Each Army helicopter carries an extensive maintenance history that accompanies the aircraft throughout its service life. Army maintenance regulations require equipment logbooks and historical maintenance files documenting inspections, repairs, component replacements and aircraft condition. These records travel with the aircraft rather than remaining solely at a particular unit, allowing commanders to monitor airworthiness and readiness over many years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-defense/department-of-the-army/rg-au/n1-au-97-011_sf115.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Aircraft Maintenance Records</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesAircraft Maintenance RecordsJune 28, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2023</span></span></span>

## Operations, Personnel and Command Records

The paperwork surrounding the crews can be as extensive as the paperwork surrounding the aircraft.

Army aviation units maintain records covering:<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://pclt.defense.gov/DIRECTORATES/Privacy-and-Civil-Liberties-Directorate/Privacy/SORNsIndex/Article/4011964/a0095-1a-tradoc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pclt.defense.gov">[pclt.defense.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pclt.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">a0095 1a tradoc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">a0095 1a tradoc</span></span></span>


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* Pilot and crew assignments.
* Flight authorisations.
* Duty rosters.
* Individual flying-hour records.
* Crew qualification and currency.
* Mission scheduling.

</div>

Army aviators also maintain official flight records because flying time affects qualification, readiness and, in many cases, flight pay. Individual flight records are separate from the aircraft maintenance records, meaning the same mission is documented from both the aircraft's perspective and the crew's perspective.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://legalclarity.org/how-to-fill-out-and-submit-da-form-2408-12-aviators-flight-record/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: legalclarity.org">[Legal Clarity]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">legalclarity.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">how to fill out and submit da form 2408 12 aviators flight record</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Legal ClarityHow to Fill Out and Submit DA Form 2408-12: Aviator’s Flight Record - LegalClarityJune 14, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 14, 2026</span></span></span>

A mission involving approximately [23 helicopters]({{ '23-helicopters/' | relative_url }}) would probably require at least several dozen pilots and crewmembers. That alone creates multiple administrative records distributed among operations offices, personnel sections and aviation units.

Command-level planning would typically generate additional documentation such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* Operations orders.
* Mission briefs.
* Radio frequencies and communications plans.
* Formation assignments.
* Emergency procedures.
* Risk assessments or operational planning documents.

</div>

Not every planning document survives indefinitely, and classified operations may use specialised handling procedures. Nevertheless, operational planning usually exists in more than one location because crews, operations personnel and command staff all require access to mission information.

## Fuel, Airfield and Flight-Following Records

Large helicopters consume substantial quantities of fuel, making fuel support one of the most visible logistical aspects of any sizeable aviation operation.

Whether aircraft depart from a military installation or a joint-use airfield, investigators would ordinarily expect supporting evidence such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* Fuel issue records.
* Refuelling vehicle logs.
* Bulk fuel accounting.
* Airfield servicing records.
* Ground crew activity reports.

</div>

Fuel accounting serves practical as well as financial purposes. Aviation fuel inventories are monitored because shortages directly affect operational readiness.

Airfield operations also produce independent records.

Depending on the mission profile, these may include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Airfield operations logs.
* Take-off and landing records.
* Flight-following documentation.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/policy/army/fm/3-04-500/appi.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: globalsecurity.org">[globalsecurity.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">globalsecurity.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>
* Communications logs.
* Base operations records.
* Air traffic coordination where required.

</div>

Military flights do not always appear in publicly accessible civilian flight databases, especially in 1980, and low-level tactical operations may not generate the same FAA documentation as civilian airline traffic. Even so, military aviation normally maintains its own operational records. ## Why Multiple Record Systems Matter

The strongest logistical argument is not that one specific document should exist, but that numerous independent record systems normally overlap.

For example:

ActivityTypical records createdAircraft preparationMaintenance logbooks, inspection documentationCrew assignmentDuty rosters, flight authorisations, crew recordsMission planningOperations orders, briefing materials, communications plansFuel supportFuel issue records, servicing documentationAirfield operationsBase operations logs, movement recordsFlight completionFlight-hour records, maintenance updates, post-flight inspections

These systems are maintained for different purposes by different personnel. Maintenance staff are concerned with aircraft condition, operations officers with mission execution, personnel offices with crew qualifications and logistics personnel with fuel and supplies.

Consequently, the absence of evidence across all of these categories would generally require more than a missing filing cabinet or a single lost report. Investigators examining any alleged large-scale military aviation operation therefore look for corroboration across multiple administrative systems rather than relying on one document alone.

## What the Cash–Landrum Investigation Found

The documentary issue became especially significant during the Army Inspector General's investigation of the Cash–Landrum case.

According to published accounts of Lieutenant Colonel George Sarran's inquiry, investigators contacted Army aviation units and attempted to identify military helicopters that could match the witnesses' descriptions. The investigation reportedly failed to locate military records placing such a formation in the relevant area on the night in question.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

That finding does not conclusively prove that no helicopters were present. Military records can be incomplete, records retention policies vary, and investigators may not have examined every conceivable source. Witnesses may also have misidentified aircraft types or numbers.

However, from a logistics perspective, the reported absence of corroborating records carries evidential weight because a mission involving roughly 23 helicopters would ordinarily be expected to generate numerous overlapping traces across maintenance, operations, personnel, fuel and airfield systems. The larger the reported formation, the stronger the expectation that at least some independent documentary evidence would survive, even if individual records were later lost or destroyed.

        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
