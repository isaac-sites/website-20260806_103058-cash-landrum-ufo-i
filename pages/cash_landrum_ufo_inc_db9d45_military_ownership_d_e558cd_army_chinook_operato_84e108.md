## Introduction

One of the most significant official enquiries in the Cash–Landrum incident was not an investigation into unidentified flying objects, but a narrowly defined search for the military helicopters that witnesses reported accompanying the luminous object. Once the aircraft descriptions pointed towards CH-47 Chinooks—an Army heavy-lift helicopter rather than an Air Force type—the Department of the Army Inspector General (DAIG) assumed responsibility for determining whether any Army, Army National Guard or Army Reserve aircraft could account for the reported formation. The resulting inquiry became an important piece of evidence because it systematically contacted the organisations most likely to have operated Chinooks in south-east Texas. Its conclusion was negative: investigators found no records or operational evidence placing Army helicopters at the reported location and time. That finding narrowed the range of plausible military explanations, but it did not independently explain what the witnesses observed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

## Why the investigation shifted from the Air Force to the Army

The Army Inspector General's involvement followed a practical rather than speculative line of reasoning. Early enquiries through Air Force channels failed to identify any Air Force aircraft matching the witnesses' descriptions. Because several witnesses described large tandem-rotor helicopters consistent with the CH-47 Chinook, responsibility naturally shifted towards the service that operated the overwhelming majority of those aircraft.

A memorandum attributed to Lieutenant Colonel George C. Sarran explains that the case reached the Department of the Army Inspector General because the Army possessed the "preponderance of troops and heavy equipment helicopters." The inquiry therefore focused on one specific question: whether Army, Army National Guard or Army Reserve helicopters had participated in the reported event. The memorandum explicitly states that investigators were **not** tasked with determining whether a UFO existed or whether the reported medical effects had another cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

This distinction is important when assessing later claims. The DAIG investigation was designed as an ownership and flight-record enquiry, not as a comprehensive investigation into every aspect of the Cash–Landrum incident.

## Commands, facilities and local units contacted

Rather than relying on a single headquarters, the Army Inspector General attempted to eliminate the most plausible military sources one by one.

According to the surviving memorandum, investigators first established the reported location—approximately 35 miles north-east of Houston near Dayton, Texas—and the reported time of approximately 2100–2130 hours on 29 December 1980. They also noted that the incident occurred during the Christmas–New Year holiday period, when Army flying activity was generally expected to be limited unless specific exercises were underway.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

## Why local aviation units mattered

The local National Guard and Reserve enquiries were especially important because they addressed one of the strongest alternative explanations.

Unlike active-duty Army aviation, National Guard aircraft may operate from state facilities while remaining federally owned and capable of federal activation. Similarly, Reserve aviation units conduct regular training flights that are separate from active-duty formations. If numerous helicopters had originated from a nearby military installation, these organisations represented some of the most likely candidates to possess relevant flight logs.

The inquiry therefore did not merely ask whether Fort Hood had aircraft in the area. It attempted to determine whether any Army-operated Chinooks or supporting helicopters from the surrounding region had records placing them near the reported location that evening.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

## What the negative replies established

After allowing time for units to review their records, the Inspector General reported that all helicopter enquiries concerning known flying activity in the relevant area were negative. No participating Army organisation identified flight records matching the reported location, time or scale of the alleged [helicopter formation]({{ 'helicopters-977f69/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

This negative result established several evidential points:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* It reduced the likelihood that the reported helicopters belonged to routine Army training operations.
* It found no support for the idea that Army National Guard Chinooks from Ellington had conducted the reported mission.
* It found no evidence that nearby Army Reserve helicopter units accounted for the sightings.
* It identified no scheduled Army manoeuvres in the area during the relevant holiday period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

Importantly, these findings are stronger than a simple statement that "the Army denied involvement." They represent the outcome of an identifiable records search conducted across the organisations most likely to have operated CH-47 aircraft in the region.

## What the investigation did not prove

The Army Inspector General's conclusions have sometimes been interpreted more broadly than the evidence supports.

The investigation did **not** conclude that witnesses fabricated the helicopters, nor did it determine what object the witnesses observed. Instead, it concluded that investigators could not connect the reported helicopters to known Army, Army National Guard or Army Reserve operations through available records.

Those are different propositions. A negative records search demonstrates an absence of corroborating military documentation within the organisations examined. It does not logically establish that no helicopters of any kind were present, nor does it identify an alternative explanation for multiple independent reports of heavy-lift helicopters. The distinction is especially relevant because the investigation's own mandate was intentionally limited to identifying possible Army aircraft rather than resolving every aspect of the Cash–Landrum incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

## How the Army search fits into the wider ownership question

Within the broader question of [military ownership]({{ 'military-denials/' | relative_url }}) and agency boundaries, the Army Inspector General enquiry represents the government's most comprehensive attempt to determine whether reported Chinooks belonged to Army-controlled aviation.

Its practical value lies less in proving what happened than in documenting what investigators actually checked. By extending enquiries beyond the Air Force and into active-duty, National Guard and Reserve aviation channels, the investigation addressed the services most likely to have operated CH-47 helicopters in south-east Texas. The absence of matching flight records therefore became meaningful negative evidence: it weakened a conventional Army aviation explanation without independently resolving the witnesses' account of the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
