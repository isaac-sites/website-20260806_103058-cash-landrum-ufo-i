## Introduction

How long did the Cash–Landrum encounter really last? The best-supported answer is that the principal close encounter probably occupied about **15 to 17 minutes**, while the wider episode—from first noticing the unusual light until the witnesses lost sight of the object and accompanying helicopters—has often been rounded to **around 20 minutes** in later retellings. Crucially, none of the three witnesses consulted a clock during the event. Every [time estimate]({{ 'time-estimate/' | relative_url }}) was reconstructed afterwards, primarily from Betty Cash's recollection that she arrived home at approximately **9:50 pm** after what she believed was a roughly **20-minute drive** from the [encounter site]({{ 'encounter-site/' | relative_url }}). That makes the timeline a retrospective estimate rather than a contemporaneous record, an important distinction when assessing both the witness testimony and later reconstructions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumint01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF interview part IJune 4, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 1996</span></span></span>

## The 9 to 9.30 pm Estimate

The commonly cited time window begins not with a recorded observation but with backwards calculation.

During the August 1981 interview at Bergstrom Air Force Base, [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}) explained that the encounter "couldn't have been that long" because she reached home at about **ten minutes to ten**, and she considered the drive from the location to her house to be approximately twenty minutes. Working backwards from those remembered figures places the end of the roadside encounter at roughly **9.30 pm**, with the initial sighting occurring sometime after **9.00 pm**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumint01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF interview part IJune 4, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 1996</span></span></span>

This reconstruction has two important limitations:

* Neither the arrival time nor the driving time was independently documented on the night.
* The witnesses did not note the time when they first saw the distant light, when they stopped [the car]({{ 'the-car/' | relative_url }}), or when they resumed driving.

As a result, the often-quoted "9.00–9.30 pm" interval should be understood as a reasonable reconstruction rather than a verified chronology. Later writers frequently present these estimates as precise timestamps even though the underlying evidence is approximate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumint01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF interview part IJune 4, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 1996</span></span></span>

## The 15-to-17-Minute Principal Sighting

Among the various duration estimates, Betty Cash's statement to Air Force investigators is the most specific.

When asked directly how long [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }}) remained over the vehicle, she replied:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* approximately **15 to 17 minutes** of exposure;
* although subjectively it "seemed like hours";
* with her estimate constrained by the remembered arrival home time rather than by any clock reading.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumint01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF interview part IJune 4, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 1996</span></span></span>

</div>

That answer is significant because it distinguishes between **experienced time** and **estimated elapsed time**. Witnesses to frightening events often report that incidents feel much longer than they objectively lasted, and Cash explicitly acknowledged this difference herself.

[Colby Landrum]({{ 'colby-landrum/' | relative_url }})'s estimate during the same interview was broader—about **15 to 20 minutes**—placing his recollection within the same general range without matching Cash's narrower figure exactly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Computer UFO Network]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Computer UFO Network Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Computer UFO NetworkTranscript, Cash-Landrum InterviewJune 10, 2002</span></span></span>

The reconstructed sequence within that period remains broadly consistent across interviews:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. A bright light is noticed ahead on the road.
2. The vehicle stops short of the object.
3. Betty Cash remains outside longer than the others while observing the object.
4. Vickie Landrum returns to comfort her grandson in the car.
5. The object ascends.
6. Multiple helicopters become visible.
7. The witnesses resume driving while continuing to observe the departing formation.

</div>

Although individual actions cannot be assigned reliable minute marks, this order of events remained comparatively stable across the principal witness accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/search/label/Colby%20Landrum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on blueblurrylines.com.</span></span></span>

## Why Later Accounts Often Round the Event to 20 Minutes

Many books, documentaries and summaries describe the Cash–Landrum encounter simply as lasting **20 minutes**.

This figure is understandable but slightly oversimplifies the original testimony.

Several factors contributed to the rounded duration:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* Betty Cash's estimated **15–17 minutes** is naturally approximated to twenty minutes in popular summaries.
* Colby Landrum independently recalled approximately **15–20 minutes**, reinforcing a rounded figure rather than a precise duration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Computer UFO Network]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Computer UFO Network Transcript, Cash-Landrum Interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Computer UFO NetworkTranscript, Cash-Landrum InterviewJune 10, 2002</span></span></span>
* Some authors measure from the first distant observation until the helicopters disappeared, while others measure only the period during which the object hovered close to the stopped vehicle.
* Secondary retellings often compress multiple witness estimates into a single easily remembered number.

</div>

Consequently, the familiar "20-minute encounter" should be viewed as a shorthand description of the overall episode rather than an exact stopwatch measurement. Later reference works frequently repeat this rounded figure while the underlying interview transcript shows a more qualified estimate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

## Why the Timeline Cannot Be Reconstructed Minute by Minute

The available evidence does not support a precise chronological reconstruction.

Several factors limit accuracy:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **No contemporaneous timing.** None of the witnesses checked a watch during the event.
* **Retrospective estimation.** The timeline depends on memories recorded weeks and months later.
* **Anchor-event reconstruction.** The remembered arrival home time became the principal reference point from which earlier events were estimated.
* **Natural memory variation.** Different interviews contain small differences in duration, sequence and descriptive detail, even while preserving the overall narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/search/label/Colby%20Landrum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[blueblurrylines.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on blueblurrylines.com.</span></span></span>

</div>

Researchers examining the original documentation have also noted that witness testimony evolved in some respects across repeated interviews, reinforcing the need to distinguish between the stable core chronology and later refinements. This does not necessarily invalidate the witnesses' recollections, but it does argue against presenting any reconstructed minute-by-minute timetable as historically established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/search/label/Colby%20Landrum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on blueblurrylines.com.</span></span></span>

## The Most Defensible Reconstruction

Using the earliest available interviews together with the August 1981 Air Force transcript produces a cautious timeline:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Approximately 9.00–9.15 pm:** the unusual light is first noticed while driving.
* **Shortly afterwards:** the vehicle stops and the principal close encounter begins.
* **Roughly 15–17 minutes:** the witnesses remain exposed to the nearby object before it departs and they continue their journey.
* **Around 9.50 pm:** Betty Cash recalls arriving home after what she estimated to be a twenty-minute drive from the encounter area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumint01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF interview part IJune 4, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 1996</span></span></span>

Within those broad limits, the evidence supports only an approximate chronology.
