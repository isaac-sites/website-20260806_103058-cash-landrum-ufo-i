## Introduction

The reported arrival of military-style helicopters is one of the most distinctive and controversial phases of the Cash–Landrum incident. According to Betty Cash, [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}) and seven-year-old Colby Landrum, the aircraft did not appear until after the fiery object had begun moving away from its low position over the road. From that point onward, they described a growing formation of helicopters surrounding or following the object as they [continued driving]({{ 'baseline-loss/' | relative_url }}), catching repeated glimpses of both through openings in the trees. This stage of the encounter transformed what had begun as a close roadside sighting into an alleged moving aerial pursuit. It also created one of the central evidential problems in the case: if such a large helicopter formation really existed, why has independent confirmation remained so limited?

## When the Aircraft First Became Visible

In the witnesses' sequence of events, the helicopters did not accompany [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }}) throughout the entire encounter. Their accounts consistently place the aircraft's arrival after the object had lifted sufficiently for [the car]({{ 'the-car/' | relative_url }}) to continue along the road.

As the object drifted away from its position over the highway, the witnesses said they resumed driving. It was during this moving phase that they first noticed helicopters approaching. Vickie Landrum later recalled that helicopters appeared to be "coming from all directions", rather than travelling as a single established formation. She described the number increasing as additional aircraft arrived.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

This timing is significant because it separates two distinct parts of the incident:

* the stationary roadside encounter beneath the object;
* the later period in which both the object and helicopters were observed while the witnesses were back inside the moving car.

Most later summaries compress these into a single dramatic scene, but the original interviews distinguish between them.

## How the Formation Was Described from the Moving Car

The witnesses never claimed they watched the helicopters continuously. Instead, they described intermittent views while driving through wooded countryside, where the object and accompanying aircraft repeatedly disappeared behind trees before becoming visible again.

Their descriptions nevertheless share several broad themes.

First, the helicopters were said to remain close to the object rather than flying independently. Betty Cash stated that they were "completely around the object", while Vickie Landrum described them as gathering around it and continuing to converge upon it. [Colby Landrum]({{ 'colby-landrum/' | relative_url }}) likewise remembered helicopters positioned above the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[cufon.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2</span></span></span>

Second, none of the witnesses described an aggressive interception. Instead of attacking or firing upon the object, they interpreted the aircraft as escorting, surrounding or accompanying it. Vickie even speculated that they looked as though they were present in case the object fell, rather than attempting to destroy it. That interpretation came from the witnesses themselves rather than from any official investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

Finally, they maintained that the helicopters remained visible for several miles. Vickie said that even as they neared Dayton they could still see the glow of the object in the distance and observed additional helicopters continuing towards it. If taken literally, this would imply that the aerial activity extended well beyond the immediate encounter site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

## Why the Reported Helicopter Count Remains Uncertain

Perhaps the best-known detail is the claim that roughly twenty-three helicopters were involved. However, the historical record shows that this figure was never entirely fixed.

The witnesses consistently acknowledged that the count was approximate.

Betty Cash told Air Force interviewers that she counted twenty-three helicopters after stopping farther along the road, but immediately added that Vickie believed she had counted a different number and that, given their physical condition and fear, some variation was unsurprising.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[cufon.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2</span></span></span>

Vickie Landrum gave a similar account. She explained that she counted twenty-two helicopters before Colby pointed out another, bringing the total to twenty-three. She also admitted that it was possible she had counted one aircraft twice, while insisting that the formation was still far larger than only a handful of helicopters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

These statements illustrate an important distinction:

* **The witnesses consistently described a large formation.**
* **The precise number varied between interviews.**

Rather than presenting an exact tally made under ideal observation conditions, the count represents a hurried estimate made at night by frightened observers watching moving aircraft.

## The Helicopter Types Described

Descriptions of the helicopters also evolved over time.

In later interviews, Vickie and Colby referred to helicopters with twin rotors, leading many later writers to identify them as Boeing CH-47 Chinook transport helicopters. However, the earliest surviving descriptions were less uniform.

Some early summaries attributed to investigator [John Schuessler]({{ 'john-schuessler/' | relative_url }}) recorded Betty Cash describing more conventional helicopters with a main rotor and tail rotor. During the later Bergstrom Air Force interview, by contrast, Betty herself also referred to aircraft with two rotors and further claimed she could see "United States Air Force" markings on them. These differences have become part of the broader debate over how witness recollections developed during repeated retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/search/label/Colby%20Landrum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on blueblurrylines.com.</span></span></span>

Because the incident occurred at night under intense lighting conditions and while the witnesses were driving, investigators have never reached consensus on exactly what helicopter models—if any—could confidently be inferred from the descriptions alone.

## Why the Helicopter Reports Became Central to the Case

The helicopter phase fundamentally changed how the incident was interpreted.

Had the witnesses reported only an unidentified luminous object, the case would have resembled many other UFO reports. The addition of a large formation of apparent military helicopters introduced a potentially testable claim. Aircraft of that size generate considerable noise, require trained crews and normally leave operational records.

Consequently, investigators attempted to determine whether military helicopter units had been operating in the region that evening. Official enquiries to the Air Force, Army and National Guard did not identify any acknowledged operation matching the witnesses' description, and no military organisation accepted responsibility for such a formation. This absence of documentary confirmation became one of the principal obstacles to establishing official involvement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wikidisc.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wikidisc.org">[WikiDisc]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wikidisc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wiki Disc Cash–Landrum incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident &#124; WikiDiscJuly 1, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2026</span></span></span>

The reported helicopters therefore occupy a unique place within the Cash–Landrum timeline. They are not merely an additional visual detail but the element that transformed the encounter into a claim involving identifiable military hardware, making the question of corroboration far more significant than it would otherwise have been.

## The Continuing Evidential Debate

The helicopter reports remain one of the most contested aspects of the incident because they combine unusually consistent core testimony with unusually difficult verification.

Several points are relatively stable across the witnesses' accounts:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the helicopters appeared after the object began moving away;
* they were observed while the witnesses were driving;
* they remained associated with the object rather than operating separately;
* the formation appeared substantially larger than only a few aircraft.

</div>

Other details are less stable, including the exact number, whether markings could genuinely be read at night, and the precise helicopter type.

Critics argue that a formation of roughly two dozen military helicopters should almost certainly have produced additional witnesses, operational records or other independent evidence. Supporters respond that the consistency of the witnesses' core descriptions across multiple interviews makes the helicopter reports difficult to dismiss simply as later embellishment. As a result, the reported formation remains one of the strongest narrative elements in the witnesses' story while simultaneously being one of the least independently corroborated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2022/10/the-cash-landrum-ufo-1980s-recording-of.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[blueblurrylines.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesBlue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO: 1980s Recording of Witnesses InterviewsOctober 7, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 7, 2022</span></span></span>

    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
