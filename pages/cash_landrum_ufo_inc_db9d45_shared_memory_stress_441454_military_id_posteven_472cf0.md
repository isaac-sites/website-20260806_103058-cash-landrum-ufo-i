## Introduction

One of the most debated details in the Cash–Landrum incident is not simply the reported presence of military helicopters, but how those helicopters came to be identified as Boeing CH-47 Chinooks. The historical record suggests that the witnesses did not immediately provide a detailed technical classification at the scene. Instead, the identification became more specific during subsequent interviews, discussions with investigators and exposure to photographs of military aircraft. This does not necessarily imply that the witnesses were mistaken or influenced improperly. Rather, it illustrates a well-documented feature of human memory: people often retain a clear visual impression while only later attaching a formal label to it. Understanding when the helicopters became "Chinooks" therefore helps distinguish between an original perception and a later classification, without assuming either deliberate fabrication or perfect recollection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

## When did the helicopters become Chinooks?

The earliest surviving descriptions focused primarily on unusual helicopters accompanying the aerial object. Witnesses consistently described numerous helicopters and repeatedly mentioned an unusual arrangement involving two rotor assemblies. However, the familiar military designation "CH-47 Chinook" became more prominent as the investigation progressed rather than appearing fully formed at the outset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

By the time of the August 1981 interview at Bergstrom Air Force Base, investigators and witnesses were openly discussing helicopter types. The transcript records officials asking whether photographs depicted the kind of helicopter the witnesses had seen. During the interview, [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}) and [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}) produced photographs they had brought with them for comparison, and discussion shifted from describing the aircraft to identifying a recognised military model.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani2.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 2 of 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 2 of 2...</span></span></span>

## What visual features witnesses may have noticed

Although technical identification developed later, several reported visual characteristics would naturally have drawn attention.

The most distinctive reported feature was the presence of two widely separated rotor systems. Few helicopters possess such a configuration, making tandem-rotor aircraft visually distinctive even to non-specialists. Witnesses repeatedly referred to what they described as a "double" rotor arrangement rather than focusing on details such as fuselage shape or dimensions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2017/09/the-cash-landrum-ufo-prime-suspect-ch.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[blueblurrylines.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO Prime Suspect: The CH-47, Army Chinook</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry Lines: The Cash-Landrum UFO Prime Suspect: The CH-47, Army Chinook</span></span></span>

## How photographs and questioning can introduce labels

Modern cognitive psychology distinguishes between recognising an object and assigning it a category or name.

A witness may accurately remember seeing an aircraft with two rotor assemblies while lacking the vocabulary to identify its official designation. Later exposure to photographs, silhouettes or investigator questions can supply that missing label. This process is known as post-event classification rather than necessarily changing the underlying visual memory.

The Bergstrom interview illustrates this distinction. Rather than relying solely on verbal recollection, investigators discussed photographs that the witnesses had brought with them. Such comparisons naturally encourage recognition by matching remembered shapes with known aircraft types.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani2.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 2 of 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 2 of 2...</span></span></span>

Memory research shows that this process can have two different outcomes:

* **Helpful recognition.** A witness correctly matches a remembered shape with a genuine aircraft.
* **Memory integration.** Features from the reference image become incorporated into the remembered event, increasing confidence in details that were originally uncertain.

The historical record cannot determine which process predominated in this particular case. It merely documents that visual comparison formed part of the identification process.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufology.patrickgross.org/htm/cashlandrumsarran01.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufology.patrickgross.org">[ufology.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufology.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: the Cash-Landrum case, 1980, USAF memo 1/4, Sarran...</span></span></span>

## Separating recognition from later classification

The distinction between recognition and classification is important because critics and supporters of the Cash–Landrum case sometimes treat them as identical.

Recognition concerns what the witnesses actually perceived:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* numerous helicopters,
* unusually large aircraft,
* tandem rotor appearance,
* military impression.

</div>

Classification concerns the later conclusion that these aircraft were specifically CH-47 Chinooks operated by a particular military service.

These are different evidential claims. Someone may sincerely recognise a distinctive silhouette while remaining uncertain about its official designation until later comparison with reference material.

Likewise, later confidence in the label does not necessarily prove that every associated detail was present in the original memory.

## Why the service branch became another point of debate

Once the helicopters were labelled as Chinooks, attention shifted to which branch of the United States military might have operated them.

Some witness statements referred to "United States Air Force" markings, while later discussions often centred on Army or Army National Guard Chinooks because the CH-47 has historically been operated primarily by the U.S. Army rather than the Air Force. This discrepancy became significant during later investigations because identifying both the aircraft type and operating service affected attempts to locate flight records and establish official responsibility.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

The service-branch issue therefore developed after, rather than before, the aircraft-type identification. It reflects an evolving investigative effort rather than an original observation recorded immediately after the incident.

## What this means for evaluating the testimony

From a psychological perspective, the progression from "large helicopters with two rotors" to "CH-47 Chinooks" is neither surprising nor uniquely suspicious.

The surviving documents indicate that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* witnesses initially described distinctive visual characteristics;
* investigators later used photographs and silhouette comparisons;
* military designations became attached during that investigative process;
* later accounts increasingly used the formal aircraft name.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[cufon.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie &amp; Colby Landrum, Part 1 of 2August 17, 1981...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 17, 1981</span></span></span>

</div>

This sequence is consistent with ordinary memory reconstruction, where perceptual impressions remain relatively stable while technical labels become more precise through later discussion. It does not establish that the helicopter identification was either correct or incorrect. Instead, it suggests that the strongest historical evidence lies in the witnesses' repeated description of unusually large tandem-rotor helicopters, while the specific classification as CH-47 Chinooks represents a later stage of interpretation built upon those original visual impressions.

  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
